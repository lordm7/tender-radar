How it works
Screen
Daily intake of public tenders. Rule filters on activity, value, region and deadline remove the obvious misses.
Score
AI rates each remaining tender 0–100 against your profile, with reasons in Arabic and English.
Decide
Booklet PDFs become a one-page brief: scope, key dates, eligibility, risks and a go / no-go checklist.
